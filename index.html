How it works

Three steps. Zero dollars.

Auralis is a single static page. There is no server, no database, no account — and nothing to pay for unless you choose a premium voice provider yourself.

01

Paste a link

Any public article or page. Auralis extracts the real article text and strips the nav, ads and clutter.

02

Pick a voice

Your device's built-in voices are free and ready now. Add an ElevenLabs or OpenAI key later if you want a studio voice.

03

Listen & ask

Follow along with word highlighting, save it to your Library, or ask one of your AI apps to summarise it.

Voices

Free voices now. Studio voices if you want them.

The free voices built into your browser and operating system already sound remarkably human — and they cost nothing. Premium voices are an optional upgrade, using a key you paste yourself. Keys are stored only in this browser's localStorage and sent only to the provider you chose.

Personal AI apps

Plug in any AI. Ask it about anything you read.

Add any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio, your own server. Each app is a card you can enable, test, use and delete. Keys stay in this browser only.

Add an AI app

OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions endpoint. Local examples: http://localhost:11434/v1 (Ollama), http://localhost:1234/v1 (LM Studio).

Use an app on the article you're reading

Open an article first, then pick an action. The article text is sent only to the app you choose, only when you tap Run.
