How it works

Three steps. Zero dollars.

Auralis is a single static page. There is no server, no database, no account — and nothing to pay for unless you choose a premium voice provider yourself.

01

Paste a link

Any public article or page. Auralis extracts the real article text and strips the nav, ads and clutter.

02

Pick a voice

Microsoft AI voices (Aria, Jenny, Guy…) are free, built in and already the default — no key, no credits. Add an ElevenLabs or Cartesia key later if you want a studio voice.

03

Listen & ask

Follow along with word highlighting, save it to your Library, or ask one of your AI apps to summarise it.

Optional · Bring your own key

Clone Studio

Clone a voice you own or have permission to use — your own voice, or a voice you made. Upload 30 seconds to 1 minute of clean sample audio and Auralis creates the clone through ElevenLabs Instant Voice Clone or Cartesia, using your own key. The clone is then listed as a voice you can use.

Only clone voices you own or have permission to use. Never download, rip or clone another company's demo audio.

Local cloning is unlimited but very slow — our own local clone runs 15–25× slower than realtime, so a full article would take hours. The Microsoft voices above are the fast unlimited option.

Personal AI apps

Plug in any AI. Ask it about anything you read.

Add any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio, your own server. Each app is a card you can enable, test, use and delete. Keys stay in this browser only.
